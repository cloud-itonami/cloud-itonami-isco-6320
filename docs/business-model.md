# Business Model: Subsistence Livestock Farm Record-Keeping and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-6320`
- ISCO-08: `6320`
- Occupation: Subsistence Livestock Farmers
- Social impact: animal-welfare-integrity, household-livelihood-continuity,
  farm-record-keeping-transparency

## Customer

- subsistence-livestock-farming households / holdings
- independent farmers/holders

## Offer

- feeding-schedule/animal-condition-check-in record logging
- household labor/task farm-operation scheduling
- feed/supplies procurement coordination
- always-escalating welfare-concern flagging channel

## Revenue

- monthly holding retainer
- per-record logging fee

## Trust Controls

- farmer/livestock-holding record must be independently verified and
  registered before any action
- no proposal, anywhere in the closed op allowlist, can finalize an
  animal-treatment/welfare/breeding decision, or override the farmer's
  own judgment about their household's livestock — these are
  structurally absent, not merely gated
- any animal-welfare, injury-risk, or livelihood-loss concern surfaces
  ONLY via `:flag-welfare-concern`, which always escalates to a human
- feed/supplies orders above the registered per-holding cost ceiling
  always require human sign-off
- work, scheduling and supply records are auditable, not editable
